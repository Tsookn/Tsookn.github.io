Feel your Time, Not Just See a List

What's New in Version 3

Rebuilt from the ground up — smoother, faster, and more fluid across every view.

  • Smoother scrolling & performance in Day, Week, Month, and List views.
  • Now in Hebrew, with full right-to-left support — more languages on the way.
  • Redesigned event view — clearer, with quick edit and navigation.
  • Smarter Event Bars plus improved automatic rule generation.
  • Rules Library — save, load, and share rule sets.
  • Two time zones per event — independent start & end (great for flights).
  • Cleaner second time zone and a live current-time marker on all views.
  • Redesigned List view focused on Today & Tomorrow.
  • Long-press anywhere — Help, Birthdays, and the second time zone.
  • Accept or decline invitations right from the app.
  • Open addresses in Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Waze.
