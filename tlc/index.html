TIMELINE CALENDAR

Feel your Time, Not Just See a List

What's New

Now on Android too — plus reminders, a home-screen widget, and six languages.

Current version: 3.30

  • Now on Android — the same Timeline Calendar, now on your Android phone (also on iPhone & iPad).
  • Reminders & Tasks — a built-in to-do list with due dates, importance and alerts, that can sync across your devices.
  • Home-screen widget — today's or tomorrow's events at a glance.
  • Six languages — English, Hebrew, French, Spanish, German and Russian, with full right-to-left support.
  • Smarter Event Bars with automatic rule generation, plus a Rules Library to save, load and share sets.
  • Two time zones per event — independent start & end (great for flights), plus a cleaner second time zone.
  • Two-zone calendar view — see the whole calendar in two time zones at once, for anyone who wants it.
  • Redesigned event & List views — clearer, with quick edit, focused on Today & Tomorrow.
  • A live current-time marker on every view, with holidays & birthdays bands.
  • Long-press anywhere — Help, Birthdays, and the second time zone.
  • Open addresses in Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Waze.
