Privacy Policy — Timeline Calendar

Last updated: July 2026

Timeline Calendar (“the app”), developed by Tsook Nir LTD, is designed with your privacy in mind. This policy applies to the app on both iOS and Android.

No personal data collected

The app does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on our servers. We have no access to your information.

Calendar access

The app reads from and writes to your device’s calendar solely to display and manage your events on your device. This calendar data stays on your device and within your own calendar accounts (for example, your Google or Apple calendar). It is never sent to us or to any third party.

On-device settings

Your preferences and settings are stored locally on your device only.

Purchases

Purchases and donations are processed by the app store (Apple App Store or Google Play). Payment is handled entirely by the store; the app does not receive or store your payment details.

No tracking or advertising

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at tlc@tsook.net.
